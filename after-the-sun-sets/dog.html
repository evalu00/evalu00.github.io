A dog barks somewhere beyond
the houses.

Another answers from farther down the
street, followed by a few more.

Most of the houses are dark, and you
can't quite tell where the sounds are
coming from.

The barking stops for a moment, leaving
only the faint rustling of leaves and the
chirping of a few songbirds still awake
at this late hour.

A Familiar Sound
